## An Update to [[My Faith Crisis]]

I have stumbled upon this channel called [An Inconvenient Faith](https://youtube.com/@inconvenientfaith?si=7wN_2Pq_Ab_Fwdqw)which goes into the stickier side of [[The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints|church]] doctrine. Going to watch all the videos they have. First one I’m watching is [episode 9](https://youtu.be/LCzsWhuZFQM?si=DULG0A3CvM12d8Ub) which touches on prophets and revelation.

I’ll report back on my thoughts once I’ve watched it all. 
##### Update:

It's apparent these videos are meant to be a neutral stance on each topic, allowing both parties to present their side of the issues presented. But while watching them I can't help but feel like the church's stance is in the wrong in most cases as they touch on such as [[LGBTQA+]], history with black members, etc. 
It's a great series and I think all church members should watch it, but I definitely don't see it as a "faith builder".
