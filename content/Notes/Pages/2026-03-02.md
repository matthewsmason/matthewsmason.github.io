## An Update to [[My Faith Crisis]]

Earlier today, [[YouTube]] brought a video forward in my feed that caught my attention. It’s only around ten minutes long, but the message really resonated with me. 

![Why Your Children Left Religion | They Aren’t Lost](https://youtu.be/j_El25qWu9E?si=oxZtwt7ZY2jnkXKQ)

[[Andrew Marusak]] on his channel [The Altarless Traveler](https://www.youtube.com/@andrewmarusak) provides what he calls “Four Fault Lines” that are the real reasons for why children are leaving religion:

* The Believability Gap.
* The Exposure Effect.
* The Integrity Gap.
* Digital Democracy.

I won't reiterate what he says in the video as I do think it's an important watch. I like all four of these points and I can see them fitting well with my own deconstruction away from the [[The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints|LDS]] faith. I'm glad I'm not alone on this journey even when it feels like it sometimes.
