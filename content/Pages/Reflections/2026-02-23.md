## An Update on [[2025-02-07|My Faith Crisis]]

I was looking up some of the talks from one of the videos I touched on earlier in [[2025-02-07|My Faith Crisis]] titled: [Latter-day Prophets Speak on Evolution](https://youtu.be/MiebbpNqpDs).

I wanted to list out the talks using Googles AI [[Gemini]] which it did surprisingly well. I'll list them below.

While re-listening and reading, what caught my attention was [[Bruce R. McConkie]]'s speech at [[Brigham Young University|BYU]] titled *The Seven Deadly Heresies*. In it, he talks about [[Evolution|evolution]]. Luckily, we have the audio recording because he says something completely different than what is written on the BYU website for the talk. In the written talk it says: 

 > “Can you harmonize these things with the evolutionary postulate that death has always existed and that the various forms of life have evolved from preceding forms over astronomically long periods of time?”
 > 
 > “Can you harmonize the theories of men with the inspired words that say: [...]”

[[McConkie_Bruce_1980_061.pdf#page=4&selection=22,14,23,45|McConkie_Bruce_1980_061, page 4]]

This is not what McConkie says at all in his talk. Listen to the talk at the 14:30 minute mark.

![[McConkie_Bruce_19802_The_Seven_Deadly_Heresies.mp3]]

It *should* read the following: 

> “*Try as you may*, you *cannot* harmonize these things with the evolutionary postulate that death always existed and that the various forms of life have evolved from preceding forms over astronomically long periods of time.”
> 
> “*Try as you may*, you *cannot* harmonize the theories of men with the inspired words that says: [...]”

I find it extremely misleading to fundamentally change Bruce R. McConkie's talk to fit the current stance of the church. This rewrite changed the meaning of his talk completely. McConkie's stance was that the belief in evolution was a heresy, a factual statement. It was not a question posed to encourage members to harmonize these two ideas as the rewritten text makes it out to be. Absolutely wild.

I need to listen to the rest of the talk and see what words and phrases they’ve tweaked compared to what was said. 

##### Update:

Looks like this has been seen before. Found a [[Reddit]] thread on the topic and it appears BYU has modified the talk quite extensively. The thread on the topic can be found [here](https://www.reddit.com/r/exmormon/comments/70an3b/did_byu_drastically_alter_bruce_r_mckonkies/).
